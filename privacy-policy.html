Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Scope & Stride ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Scope & Stride mobile application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account information: name and email address
  • Horse records: health data, service visits, farrier and vet records
  • Billing information: invoices and payment history (payment card data is processed by Stripe and never stored by us)
  • Usage data: standard app analytics and crash reports
  • User content: posts, photos, and comments you share within your barn's feed

How we use it

  • To operate and improve the app
  • To send you notifications about barn activity and alerts
  • To generate and deliver invoices
  • To provide customer support

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app (Supabase for database and authentication, Stripe for payments, Resend for email, Apple for push notifications).

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us at hello@scopeandstride.com.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Authentication is handled by Supabase.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@scopeandstride.com.
